Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Slicer Companion ("the App") is developed by Emergent. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

The App collects and processes the following data solely to provide its core functionality:

  • 3D Model Files (STL) — Files you upload are stored on our servers to enable slicing and preview features. These files are not shared with third parties.
  • Printer and Filament Profiles — Configuration data you create (printer names, build volumes, filament types, temperatures) is stored to persist your settings.
  • Print Settings and Presets — Slice parameters you configure are stored for reuse across sessions.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not collect device identifiers or analytics
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not access your camera or photo library

Local Network Access

The App may access your local network to discover and communicate with 3D printers running OctoPrint or Moonraker. This communication happens directly between your device and your printer. No local network data is sent to our servers.

Data Storage and Security

Your data (uploaded STL files, printer profiles, filament profiles, and presets) is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data in transit and at rest.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you use the App. You can delete individual models, printers, filaments, and presets at any time from within the App. Deleted data is permanently removed from our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App does not share your data with any third-party services, analytics providers, or advertisers.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@slicercompanion.app
